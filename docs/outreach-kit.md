**Send to (in this order, one at a time, wait ~4 days between):**

| Outlet | Where to send |

| Urban List Sydney | story tips form |
| news.com.au lifestyle | tips form (cost-of-living angle) |

| ABC Radio Sydney (texture/talkback) | ABC Sydney contact form - mornings producer |

>

## Notes

- **One story landed ≈ +5% to the site's entire current link profile** (~15-25 referring
  domains today). This is the highest-leverage 20 minutes available.
- Never pay for links; never ask for specific anchor text (both look manipulative).
- When a story lands, add an "As featured in" line to /visit-bondi-beach (the About page).
- The Coffee Index re-run each year = a fresh pitch each year ("Bondi coffee up X% since 2026").
